    Help

    Short answers, kept honest.

    How monitoring works

    • Pick an interval — steady, or a random wait between a shortest and longest value.
    • Pick a stop condition. Refreshing continues while the condition is unmet.
    • Page text comparisons ignore the areas you name in Ignore changes inside, which keeps clocks and ads from stopping your watch.
    • A limit of 0 refreshes or 0 minutes means no limit at all.
    • Preserve scroll keeps your reading position; bypass cache asks the site to reload without cached files.

    Keyboard and focus

    • Tab and Shift+Tab move through the popup in reading order; every control has a visible focus ring.
    • Give each radio button or checkbox focus and press Space to choose it; press Enter in a text field to start.
    • Element picking closes the popup so you can click the page. Reopen the popup and the selector is filled in.

    When something does not work

    • Browser-internal pages (chrome://), the Chrome Web Store, other extensions, and pages you cannot script cannot be refreshed.
    • Refreshing reloads the page, so unsaved form edits are lost. Save your work first.
    • Sessions do not survive a browser restart; they are cleared rather than silently resumed.
    • A changed origin ends monitoring, because access is granted per tab and site.

    Permissions in plain words

    • activeTab — temporary access to the tab you start, not to every site you visit.
    • scripting — adds the local monitoring script and element picker to that tab.
    • storage — keeps your settings, profiles, and temporary session state.
    • alarms — wakes the extension so scheduling keeps working when the background worker sleeps.

    Free, and staying that way

    Every feature described here is included for everyone. There is no paid tier, no trial timer, no locked setting, and no upsell in the popup or on this page.

    Privacy

    Short version: your browsing stays on your device.

    • No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking libraries.
    • No page contents, keywords, URLs, or results are sent to a developer or to any server.
    • Settings and profiles are stored in local extension storage until you delete them or uninstall the extension.
    • Active sessions live in temporary session storage and are cleared when the browser session ends.
    • The extension has no external endpoints of its own; refreshing only makes the website's normal requests.
    • Avoid saving private text as a keyword profile on a shared computer.

    The full policy ships with the source repository as docs/PRIVACY.md. Uninstalling the extension removes its browser-managed local storage.
